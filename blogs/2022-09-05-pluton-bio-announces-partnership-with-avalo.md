Pluton Biosciences and Avalo have signed an agreement to apply machine learning to Pluton’s exclusive Micromining platform to accelerate development of microbial carbon capture products.  Micromining delivers huge microbial, and associated metagenomic data sets to develop novel microbial carbon capture products.
